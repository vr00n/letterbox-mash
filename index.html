Mathematical Breakdown

Pearson Correlation ?
In plain English "Do you agree on which films are great vs. bad?" Even if one of you rates everything harshly and the other generously, this still detects if your opinions trend in the same direction. +1 = perfect agreement, −1 = total opposites.
--
Adjusted Cosine ?
In plain English Measures if your overall taste "direction" matches. Corrects for rating style — a harsh critic and a generous one can still score high here if they love and hate the same films relatively.
--
Jaccard Overlap ?
In plain English How much of your watchlists overlap? This ignores ratings entirely — it just asks: "what fraction of the films you've each seen did you BOTH see?" Higher = you've watched more of the same movies.
--
Vector Distance ?
In plain English The raw gap between every single rating in your two profiles. Think of it as the "physical distance" between your taste fingerprints in movie space. Lower = closer. Zero would mean identical ratings on everything.
